## Priority

1. The existing Stripe checkouts on www.barrandodger.com stay the only live payment buttons. They still do not email a file. The three titles do not have a verified finished manuscript, so nothing new was attached.
2. Payhip is the first new channel to open when a finished private file exists. Its FAQ says the free plan charges 5% of the sale, plus the card processor. It delivers a digital file. No monthly fee was stated for that free plan.
3. Gumroad is a second direct-download option. Its help page says 10% + US$0.50 on direct sales, plus card processing, and 30% on Discover sales. No monthly fee. It costs more than Payhip's free plan, so it was not opened.
4. Ko-fi Shop is 5% on shop sales and can pay into the seller's own Stripe or PayPal. Not opened.
5. Lemon Squeezy is 5% + US$0.50, with extra fees outside the US. It would be a second merchant of record. Not opened.
6. Kindle, Draft2Digital and Kobo are ebook shops, not private PDF delivery. They need an account, an EPUB, and a finished book. KDP Select must not be used: the texts are already public.
7. Shopify was not priced and was not opened. A monthly shop is not justified yet.
8. Print (IngramSpark, Lulu) was not priced. No print file exists.

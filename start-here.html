What this archive is

The Barran Dodger archive is a public documentary record of testimony, official correspondence, and related materials. A link is not a finding. An allegation remains an allegation until examined against the underlying record.

Two companion sites

  • Rebuild archive — the reconstructed reading interface you are currently on.
  • Barran Dodger archive — the principal public documentary record.

How to navigate

  • Existing dated statements and PDFs remain exactly as published.
  • Look for original documents rather than relying only on narrative pages.
  • Hashes and OpenTimestamps receipts help show that a particular file has not changed; they do not prove the truth of its contents.

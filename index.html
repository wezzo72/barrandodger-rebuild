05 · ACCOUNTABILITY

Evidence first.

Accountability begins with making the underlying record inspectable: who communicated, what was said, what response followed, what documentary material supports each proposition, and what remains unresolved.

ACCOUNTABILITY / 001

Official Correspondence

Government, agency and institutional communications can be indexed and connected to the records to which they relate.

ACCOUNTABILITY / 002

Claims & Evidence

Claims should remain distinguishable from documentary evidence, interpretation and conclusions drawn from the record.

ACCOUNTABILITY / 003

Contradictions

Conflicting statements, discrepancies and unexplained gaps can be identified for examination.

ACCOUNTABILITY / 004

Primary Sources

Wherever possible, narratives should point directly back to the underlying preserved material.

ACCOUNTABILITY / 005

Official Decisions

Responses and decisions can be documented without removing the context in which they were made.

ACCOUNTABILITY / 006

Unresolved Questions

Questions for which the currently preserved material does not provide a complete answer remain explicitly identified as such.

06 · JUSTICE & REMEDY

From preservation to action.

Preservation is the foundation. This section provides a structured place to document outstanding matters, requested remedies, supporting material and avenues through which appropriate independent assistance may be sought.

WHAT IS BEING SOUGHT

A clear statement of requested assistance, remedies, accountability measures and practical outcomes.

OUTSTANDING MATTERS

A structured record of questions and issues that remain unresolved within the documentary record.

INDEPENDENT EXAMINATION

Material organised so that journalists, researchers, lawyers, advocates and other appropriate reviewers can examine the underlying records.

PRESERVATION

Continued preservation protects the documentary record and helps maintain access to material over time.

The archive does not itself determine legal liability or establish a judicial finding. Its purpose is to preserve, organise and make the documentary material accessible for examination.

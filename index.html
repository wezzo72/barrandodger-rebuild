A STATEMENT FROM THE BARRAN DODGER ARCHIVE

Truth, like light, does not need permission to shine or permission to exist; lies require constant maintenance, repetition and concealment. My truth only needed time. And now, by God’s grace, that time has come. Through decades of poverty, isolation, homelessness, excommunication, erasure and what I experienced as almost total human abandonment, my existence was nevertheless preserved long enough for me to endure, remember, collect, collate and publish more than three decades of evidence.

Brick by brick, the record was made from the words, decisions, correspondence and official documents of the very institutions whose actions are now capable of being examined. What was scattered across years, offices, agencies and governments can now be placed beside itself and examined as a whole.

The record does not require me to manufacture a story; the record speaks in its own language. That is the paradox: systems that produced documents that once helped contain, dismiss, obscure or erase my experience also produced the documentary trail by which those actions can now be examined.

Time did not destroy the evidence. Time preserved it.

And now the archive exists.

04 · PRESERVATION METHOD

What was preserved?

According to the archive documentation, a raw download of the original site could leave only an empty shell because some content was produced by JavaScript. The preservation process therefore captured pages after their content had rendered.

METHOD / 001

Rendered Pages

Pages were captured after their own JavaScript had rendered so that the resulting HTML contained the visible documentary material.

METHOD / 002

Multiple Formats

Each page was retained as rendered HTML, PDF and a separate hyperlink record.

METHOD / 003

Metadata

The manifest records information including page identifiers, titles, character counts, link counts and hashes.

METHOD / 004

Finding Aid

The archive is structured as a finding aid rather than presented as a court finding or legal verdict.

05 · IMPORTANT

Read the source.

The archive preserves documentary material for examination. Readers should distinguish source records from claims, allegations, interpretation and conclusions, and should consult the underlying material wherever possible.

FINDING AID · NOT A COURT · NOT A VERDICT

This archive organises and preserves documentary material. It does not itself constitute a judicial determination.

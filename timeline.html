01 · Chronology

The record unfolds over time.

The timeline is designed to distinguish chronology from interpretation. Events are presented as points in the documentary record, with links back to the underlying material wherever appropriate.

CHRONOLOGICAL PRINCIPLE Dates and events should be understood in the context of the underlying documents. Where the available record does not establish a precise date or sequence, that uncertainty should remain visible rather than being filled by assumption.

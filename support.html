01 · THE PURPOSE

Support the record.

The central purpose of this archive is preservation. Supporting the project means helping keep the underlying documentary material accessible, searchable and available for independent examination.

Support does not require accepting every claim contained within the archive. Independent examination, corroboration and disagreement are compatible with preservation of the underlying record.

02 · HOW TO HELP

Practical support.

Preserve

Keep copies of important documents, correspondence and other primary records so they remain available for future examination.

Verify

Compare claims against primary documents and identify corroborating or contradictory evidence wherever possible.

Document

Record dates, sources, correspondence and outcomes carefully so that later examination can follow the documentary sequence.

Review

Read the underlying material rather than relying solely upon summaries, interpretations or secondary descriptions.

Share responsibly

When sharing material, preserve its source and context and distinguish documented facts from allegations or interpretation.

Identify gaps

Missing documents, uncertain dates and unanswered questions are useful findings when they are clearly identified and preserved.

05 · DOCUMENTARY STANDARD

Preserve context.

A useful documentary record should make it possible for another person to locate the source, understand its context and reach their own assessment.

The archive is a finding aid and preservation resource. It is not a court, not a verdict and not a substitute for independent investigation, corroboration or formal legal determination.

06 · THE RECORD REMAINS

Preservation is participation.

The most durable form of support is ensuring that the documentary record remains accessible, traceable and capable of being independently examined.

1. The Witness Remains — Evidence, Survival and the Demand for Accountability

Master Documentary Edition. The cover calls it the Barran Dodger Master Humanitarian Appeal, and says it is a documentary finding aid, not a court and not a verdict.

What is in it: an opening statement; a personal declaration of faith and witness; an essay on preservation, including what a blockchain timestamp can and cannot prove; a note on the literary and prophetic titles; and a front statement of three issues the author asks recipients to examine — corruption, administration of office, and harm to people. The opening statement presents an illustrative public-cost scenario of $10.5 million over 35 years and says that figure is a model to be tested against records, not an established total. About 106 pages. This copy’s PDF metadata has no creation date. The date in the filename is the day it was added here.

An email of 18 October 2026, 7:14 pm Sydney, from Rich McLean. The subject line is MASTER COMMAND — COMPLETE CONSOLIDATED FORMAL NOTICE. The filename of this copy is marked 5. About 85 pages.

3. OPMC — the letter to the OAIC

A PDF of email correspondence. The outer message is a forward of 14 July 2022 from Rich McLean, subject “Fwd: Hayden... The OPMC cover up from OAIC”. Inside it is a letter of 23 May 2022 to Summen Sarwar at the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, about a freedom-of-information matter involving the Office of the Prime Minister and Cabinet.

The letter is the author’s account. It names people and agencies and makes allegations, including allegations of serious crime against a named private person. Those allegations are not findings. The PDF also contains images the author attached to that email, including a photograph of a door and screenshots of messages and a hosting notice.

01 · Overview

What this record contains.

The Barrandodger archive brings together a large documentary record relating to the author's experiences, communications with public institutions, official responses, supporting material and subsequent analysis.

The purpose of this section is not to substitute a conclusion for the evidence. It is to provide a clear route through the record so that individual claims can be examined against the documents that accompany them.

DOCUMENTARY PRINCIPLE The underlying records are preserved separately from this presentation. Interpretations, claims and conclusions should be distinguished from the underlying documentary material and tested against the available evidence.

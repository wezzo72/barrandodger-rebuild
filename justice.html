DOCUMENTARY ARCHIVE · JUSTICE

JUSTICE.

A structured record of the remedies, recognition, accountability and unresolved issues identified within the Barrandodger documentary record.

01 · PURPOSE

What justice means in the record.

Justice can involve recognition of what occurred, access to an appropriate process, correction of an inaccurate record, accountability where warranted, and appropriate remedy where harm is established.

This page records matters raised by the documentary archive. It does not determine whether a legal wrong has occurred or whether a particular remedy is legally available. Those questions depend upon evidence, applicable law and the relevant decision-making process.

02 · REMEDIES

What can a remedy involve?

Recognition

Acknowledging the existence and significance of documented events, correspondence, decisions or unresolved issues.

Correction

Where an official record is demonstrated to be inaccurate or incomplete, appropriate correction or clarification may become an issue for examination.

Accountability

Where evidence establishes that conduct requires review, the appropriate accountability mechanism can be identified and pursued.

Redress

Where harm is established, the relevant legal or administrative framework may determine whether compensation or another form of redress is available.

Independent Review

Matters that cannot be resolved from the existing record may require examination by an appropriate independent body.

Preservation

Preserving the documentary record ensures that future examination does not depend solely upon recollection or incomplete summaries.

03 · DOCUMENTARY PATH

From record to remedy.

01

ESTABLISH THE RECORD

Identify the primary documents, chronology, correspondence and other material relevant to the issue.

02

TEST THE CLAIM

Compare assertions with the underlying evidence and distinguish documented facts from statements, interpretation and allegation.

03

Determine which legal, administrative, review or accountability process, if any, is relevant to the issue.

04

Present the established record and the requested remedy to the appropriate decision-maker or review mechanism.

05

PRESERVE THE RESULT

Preserve subsequent correspondence, decisions and outcomes alongside the existing documentary record.

06 · DOCUMENTARY LIMIT

The archive records. It does not adjudicate.

Preservation allows the evidence to remain available for examination. Determining liability, entitlement, compensation or other legal consequences belongs to the appropriate independent process.

The archive is a finding aid and preservation resource. It is not a court, not a verdict and not a substitute for independent legal or factual determination.

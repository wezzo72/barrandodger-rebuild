04 · Verification

Preservation is separate from interpretation.

The purpose of the archive is to preserve a documentary record. The existence of a preserved document does not, by itself, establish the truth of every claim made within that document.

Evidence should therefore be distinguished from interpretation, allegation, inference and conclusion. The preserved material is provided so that those distinctions can be examined rather than hidden.

DOCUMENTARY PRINCIPLE The archive is a finding aid and preservation record. It is not presented as a court, verdict or independent determination of the truth of every assertion contained within the archived material.
